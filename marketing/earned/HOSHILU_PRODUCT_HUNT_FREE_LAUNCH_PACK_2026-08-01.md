HOSHILU turns the details you remember—such as color, shape, purpose, or where you saw an item—into a product search. It covers five core marketplaces and adds five more only for fashion searches, without requiring an exact product name.

**First maker comment**  
We built HOSHILU for a common shopping problem: remembering what a product looks like or does, but not knowing its name. Describe the clues you have and start a cross-marketplace search across Amazon, Rakuten, Yahoo Shopping, Qoo10, and SHEIN, with ZOZOTOWN, SHOPLIST, MUSINSA, BUYMA, and SNKRDUNK added only for fashion searches. The service is free to try, and we ask users not to enter personal information. HOSHILU presents at most one verified product-detail URL per marketplace and does not treat unverified links as revenue links. Prices, availability, and delivery terms should always be confirmed on the linked marketplace page. We would especially value feedback on which clues make a difficult product easiest to find.
