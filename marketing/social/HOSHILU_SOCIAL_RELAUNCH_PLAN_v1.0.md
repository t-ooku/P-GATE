# HOSHILU Social Relaunch Plan v1.0

## Objective

Make `ホシる` a recognizable action among younger users:

- `これ、ホシルで探して`
- `気になったから、ほしっトク`
- `値下げしたらホシっといて`

The first 30 days optimize for participation and repeat use, not follower count alone.

## Account migration

| Channel | Current | Target | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | renamed | `hoshilu.app` | Confirm profile image, bio, production link, and pinned posts |
| X | random current handle | `@hoshilu` → `@hoshilu_jp` | Change handle, display name, image, bio, header, and pinned post |
| TikTok | not created | `@hoshilu` → `@hoshilu_jp` | Create after 10 short-form masters are ready |
| YouTube | not confirmed | `@hoshilu` → `@hoshilu_jp` | Reserve handle and publish Shorts |
| LINE | integration pending | `ホシル｜欲しいを一緒に見つける` | Rename before webhook launch |

Do not delete an existing account. Rename it where possible so account age, permissions,
and audit history remain intact. Keep screenshots of the old profile and record the change date.

## Launch content pillars

| Share | Pillar | Example |
|---:|---|---|
| 35% | 名前が分からないクイズ | `これ、なんて検索する？` |

| 10% | ホシっといて | price drop, restock, launch alerts |

## 30-day KPI

- Profile-to-site click rate
- Query started
- Search completed
- `ほしっトク` save rate
- Seven-day return rate
- Share rate and comment participation
- Seller-page click-through

Reach and follower count are supporting metrics, not the sole success criteria.

## User-operated steps

2. Rename existing accounts instead of deleting them.
